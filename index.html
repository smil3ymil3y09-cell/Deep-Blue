EXPEDITION BRIEFING

Collect yellow crates during your collection phase, then return to the glowing station. Ship research extends timers, hull, emergency braking, automatic repair and drilling capacity. Ordinary crates contain 1 gold. Golden formations with multiple valuable crates wait at 3000 distance; obsidian formations unlock at 10000 and magma formations at 20000, at a random bearing each run, inside a connected 56-pixel-wide golden asteroid labyrinth, with a clear zone that excludes ordinary asteroids. Use the on-screen joystick on mobile or your mouse on desktop to accelerate; coasting slows gently. Equip Emergency Brake in an ability slot, then press its number (slot 10 = 0) to stop immediately. Equip Asteroid Tracker to display the nearest uncleared Golden, Obsidian and Magma formations; collecting every valuable crate in a formation clears it and switches tracking to the next nearest. HAL automatically repairs hull during expeditions. Hostile red-square outposts spawn at least 2,000 units apart, fire bursts of 10 bright projectiles over 3 seconds within 1,000 units, then pause for 2 seconds, and shut down when rammed without collision damage. Fired bullets continue travelling after shutdown until they leave the screen. Dodge is free: press Space (or DODGE on mobile) to lunge forward with 0.30s invulnerability against both asteroids and bullets, initially on a 30s cooldown. BONUSES research improves cooldown, invulnerability and distance. Your gold is banked only when you return safely. Your furthest distance each run gives a free gold multiplier: 1000 distance = ×1, 1345 = ×1.345. Gold is stored to three decimal places, always rounded down.
